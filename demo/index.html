V

THE MAKING READY

Make ready Form, and bear it with you.

MAC → XCODE → IPHONE

THE MAKER'S INSCRIPTION

Provide first a Mac and an Apple Account; provide also an iPhone bearing iOS 17 or any later issue; and through Xcode shall Form be brought forth upon the device.

TAKE UP THE SOURCE ↗
  1. I
    THE SOURCE

    Take up the work at its beginning.

    Install Xcode, draw Form down from GitHub, and open Form.xcodeproj.

  2. II
    THE SEAL

    Set your name upon the work.

    Choose your Personal Team and give the app a bundle identifier belonging to no other.

  3. III
    THE JOINING

    Bind the iPhone to the Mac.

    Grant Trust, choose the device as the destination, and awaken Developer Mode if required.

  4. IV
    THE AWAKENING

    Give the command to Run.

    Xcode shall build Form and set it upon the iPhone.

So the course returns.
